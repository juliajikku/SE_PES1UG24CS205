CSE D SECTION
SCENARIO NO. 18 (SUBJECT RECRUITMENT ENGINE FOR CLINICAL TRIALS)




ID
	Type
	Description
	Priority
	Acceptance Criteria
	Rationale
	FR-001
	Functional
	The system shall evaluate candidate pre-screening responses against defined trial protocol inclusion/exclusion criteria.
	High
	Pass: Candidate failing >=1 exclusion criteria is marked "Ineligible" with reasons logged.
Fail: Ineligible candidates marked "Eligible" or failed criteria are omitted.
	Prevents unqualified subjects from enrolling, protecting trial validity and patient safety.
	FR-002
	Functional
	The system shall provide an electronic Informed Consent Form (eICF) workflow with digital signature capture and version tracking.
	High
	Pass: Timestamped signature captured; candidate status updates to "Consented"; PDF copy sent.
Fail: Unsigned forms allow progression or consent version is untracked.
	Compliance with GCP (Good Clinical Practice) and legal mandates for human subject trials.
	FR-003
	Functional
	The system shall enable Clinical Researchers to configure trial protocol criteria (demographics, biomarkers, medical history).
	Medium
	Pass: Researcher creates/updates criteria; updates reflect immediately in the evaluation engine.
Fail: Criteria changes fail to persist or do not update real-time screening rules.
	Protocols evolve across phases; researchers need dynamic rule configuration without code redeployment.
	FR-004
	Functional
	The system shall log and track subject clinical trial visit compliance milestones (scheduled, completed, missed, rescheduled).
	Medium
	Pass: Researcher logs visit outcome; missed visit triggers an alert and compliance rate recalculation.
Fail: Visit status updates fail to save or do not update overall subject compliance score.
	Ensures protocol adherence and identifies non-compliant subjects risking study attrition.
	FR-005
	Functional
	The system shall notify eligible candidates and assignees via automated SMS/email upon status change or upcoming visit.
	Low
	Pass: Notification dispatched within 60 seconds of eligibility classification or milestone trigger.
Fail: Failed delivery without retry mechanism or incorrect subject notified.
	Minimizes subject drop-off and maintains recruitment velocity.
	NFR-001 
	Non-Functional
	The system must comply with FDA 21 CFR Part 11 and HIPAA standards via immutable cryptographic audit trails and AES-256 encryption at rest. 
	High
	Pass: All CRUD actions on subject data log an immutable entry (User ID, timestamp, prior/new value); zero plaintext PII.
Fail: Missing audit entries for data modifications or plaintext storage of PHI.


	Regulatory rejection and massive penalties occur if trial data integrity or patient privacy is breached. 
	NFR-002 
	Non-Functional
	The eligibility screening engine must process and classify candidate surveys under peak load within sub-second latency, maintaining 99.9% uptime. 
	Medium 
	Pass: 95th percentile response time <800 ms under 500 concurrent survey submissions.
Fail: p95>=800 ms or error rate >0.1% during load testing


	Prevents candidate drop-off during high-traffic campaign recruitments and ensures trial continuity.
	



Actors: Trial Candidate, Clinic Researcher, SMS notification, recieptionist
